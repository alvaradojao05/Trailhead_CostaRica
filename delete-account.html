Trailhead to Costa Rica — Delete Your Account

Last updated: 2026-10-31

This page explains how to delete your Trailhead to Costa Rica account and the data associated with it. Trailhead to Costa Rica is an independent app developed by David Alvarado Oviedo.

Option 1 — Delete your account in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Trailhead to Costa Rica app.
  2. Tap the gear icon (Settings) in the top-right corner.
  3. If you are not signed in, tap "Sign in / Create account" and sign in.
  4. In Settings, tap "Delete account".
  5. Confirm. Your account and profile are permanently deleted.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email trailheadcostarica@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify and delete your account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity (email address).
  • Your profile and display name.
  • Your community forum posts and topics are removed or disassociated from you.

What data is kept, and for how long

  • Deletion is immediate when done in the app (Option 1), and within 30 days for email requests (Option 2).
  • We may retain limited records (for example, purchase or subscription records) only as long as required by law, tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention obligations. These are not used to identify you after deletion.
  • Subscription and payment records are also held by the app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) and our payment provider (RevenueCat) under their own retention policies.
  • Any AI provider API key you entered was stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app; we never received it.
