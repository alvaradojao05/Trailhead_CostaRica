Frequently asked questions

How does the free version work?

You can use the first two sections ("Still deciding?" and "On my way") with their preset questions for free, no account needed. Each answer includes its source.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks open questions to the AI assistant, the full community forum (read, post, and reply), and an ad-free experience. It is available as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Trailhead to Costa Rica.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Premium screen in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same account used for the original purchase.

How do I report or block someone in the forum?

Tap the ⋯ button on any post to report objectionable content or block a user. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content or users that violate our community rules.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Sign out area → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and associated data.
