Trailhead to Costa Rica — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Trailhead to Costa Rica ("the app", "we") helps people considering or planning a move to Costa Rica. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Email address — used to sign you in.
  • Display name — shown next to your forum posts.
  • Forum content — the topics and posts you write.
  • App settings — language, provider choice and preferences.

Any AI provider API key you enter is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

How we use your data

To provide your account, the community forum, and app features. We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising profiles.

Storage and third parties

  • Account and forum content are stored on Supabase (our backend provider).
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive only basic profile info (email, name) to create your account.
  • If you connect your own AI provider, your questions are sent to that provider under their own terms and privacy policy.
  • Purchases are processed by the app stores and RevenueCat.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete account, which permanently removes your account and profile. For other data requests, contact us via Settings → Request a feature.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via Settings → Request a feature in the app.
